You live in Paris, Montreal or London and want to buy in Douala? You can do it without flying over at every stage, provided you are methodical. Distance is not the real risk. The real risk is paying without seeing, or handing everything to a relative with nothing in writing. Here are the steps we follow with our buyers in the diaspora.

## 1. Define your plan and your budget

A home for your stays, a place for your parents, a property to let: the plan determines the neighbourhood and the type of property. On top of the price, allow for notary fees and transfer costs. Ask for a written estimate from the very first conversation.

## 2. Check the documents before any payment

Ask for the land title, a recent certificate of ownership and, for a building, the building permit. Copies can be sent to you, then checked on the ground by a notary of your choice. Pay no deposit before that check.

## 3. Visit, even remotely

A video-call tour shows the land, the street, the building site and the surroundings. Ask to see the boundary markers, the access road and the utilities. If a relative goes on your behalf, give them a checklist.

## 4. Sign through a power of attorney if needed

If you cannot come to sign, you can give a power of attorney to someone you trust. Put it in writing, for one specific transaction, and have it legalised according to the formalities in force in your country of residence. The notary will tell you what form is required.

## 5. Pay through traceable channels

Pay by bank transfer, to the notary’s or the developer’s account, and keep every receipt. A schedule tied to the progress of the works protects you: you pay for what has been built. Refuse any cash payment through a go-between.

## 6. Follow the build

Insist on regular news: completion rate, dated photos, milestones reached. On this website, the Progress page is updated every month. You can also ask for a video tour at any time.

## 7. Prepare for handover

On completion, a handover inspection is the time to note any snags. You, or your representative, sign the handover report and receive the keys. Think ahead now: moving in, letting, or management by a professional.
